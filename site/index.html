DengueCast BD

Probabilistic forecasts of weekly dengue hospital admissions in Bangladesh, 1-4 weeks ahead, from public data, evaluated under a pre-registration. Not a clinical tool. Data: DGHS; Mendeley z5mvwrfgsz v1 (Naher 2026, CC BY 4.0).

Data quality

2022-2023 weekly series (9 units, 936 unit-weeks): recovered 0, missing 0, flagged as outliers (rule 9) 167. Source: results/data_report/weekly_2022_2023.csv.

Limitations

  • Targets are hospital admissions reported by DGHS, not infections.
  • Only one season (2024) is a true test; 2023 results are validation and therefore optimistic.
  • The 2022-2024 data (Mendeley z5mvwrfgsz) has no separate Dhaka city corporations and no national series; national values in the backtest are the sum of the eight divisions (amendment D10).
  • Weather and population features are not used yet.
  • Reporting coverage has changed over time; serotype shifts and immunity are not in public data.
  • Division level is too coarse to target mosquito control within cities.
  • Live forecasts cannot be scored yet; no claim of live accuracy is made.
  • The first live forecast (origin 24 Oct 2026) was published late; see docs/DEVIATIONS.md.
