final report and pitch. Pre-lock amendments D1-D11 are in
`ASSUMPTIONS_AND_BLOCKERS.md` and consolidated in `preregistration.md`.

| 2026-10-27 | **Reprint dating rule and archive correction.** Measured on 2026 reprints, articles published 12:00-23:59 BDT matched other sources' same-day total 107/107 times, but articles published before noon were usually the previous day's bulletin (15/19). Morning articles are now rejected as date-ambiguous. Re-running the collection with this rule (plus Daily Waadaa, Bangladesh Pratidin and Dhaka Tribune sitemaps; guards against measles bulletins and single-hospital stories) showed that 17 previously ingested dates rested on morning articles or no longer passed the checks (e.g. 11 and 14 Sep had been the previous day's bulletins). They were withdrawn with 135 appended retraction rows (empty value, reason in the note) and the corrected values appended; nothing was edited. No published forecast used the withdrawn rows. | Date-assignment error found on review. | live forecasts from origin 2026-10-31 |
