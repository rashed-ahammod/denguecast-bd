Health 5 more die of dengue, 1,935 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: October 8, 2026, 06:36 PM
Five more people died of dengue in the past 24 hours, while 1,935 new patients have been admitted to hospitals with the mosquito-borne disease.
The figures were released today (Thursday) in the Directorate General of Health Services’ (DGHS) regular dengue report issued by its Health Emergency Operation Centre and Control Room.
According to the report, among those admitted in the past 24 hours, 382 patients were from Dhaka Division outside the city corporations, 292 from Khulna Division, 280 from Chattogram, 233 from Dhaka South City Corporation, 206 from Rajshahi, 195 from Barishal Division, 175 from Dhaka North City Corporation, 93 from Mymensingh, 64 from Rangpur and 15 from Sylhet.
According to DGHS data, a total of 294 people have died from dengue in the country so far this year. During the same period, 94,151 dengue patients have been admitted to hospitals nationwide.
In the past 24 hours, 2,057 patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals. This brings the total number of dengue patients who have recovered and returned home after treatment this year to 88,208.
