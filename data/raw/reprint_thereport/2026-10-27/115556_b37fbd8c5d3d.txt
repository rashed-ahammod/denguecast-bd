Health Dengue claims 7 more lives, 1,872 hospitalised in Bangladesh The Report Desk
Published: October 17, 2026, 09:04 PM
Photo: Collected
Dengue has claimed seven more lives in Bangladesh, while 1,872 people were admitted to hospitals with the mosquito-borne disease in the 24 hours to 8am on Saturday, according to the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
Dhaka division recorded the highest number of new hospitalisations, with 865 patients. Chattogram reported 260 cases, followed by Khulna with 233, Barishal with 199, Rajshahi with 176, Mymensingh with 67, Rangpur with 59 and Sylhet with 13.
Since the beginning of this year, 110,312 people have been hospitalised with dengue across the country. Of them, 104,199 have recovered and been discharged, while 5,771 remain under treatment.
The latest deaths have taken the nationwide dengue death toll to 342 this year, the DGHS said in a press release on Saturday.
