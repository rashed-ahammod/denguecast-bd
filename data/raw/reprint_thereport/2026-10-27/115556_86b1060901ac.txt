Health 6 more die of dengue, 1,608 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: October 3, 2026, 05:32 PM
Six more people died of dengue in the past 24 hours, while 1,608 new dengue patients have been admitted to hospitals during the same period.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) disclosed the information in its regular dengue report on Saturday.
The report did not provide a divisional breakdown of the six dengue deaths recorded in the past 24 hours.
Among the newly hospitalised patients, 171 were admitted from Dhaka North City Corporation, 223 from Dhaka South City Corporation and 131 from areas outside city corporations in Dhaka Division.
In addition, 319 patients were admitted in Chattogram Division, 276 in Khulna Division, 132 in Rajshahi Division, 215 in Barishal Division, 79 in Mymensingh Division and 62 in Rangpur Division.
According to DGHS data, a total of 256 people have died from dengue in the country between January 1 and October 3 this year. During the same period, 84,369 dengue patients have been hospitalised, of whom 78,482 have recovered and been discharged.
