Health Dengue claims 7 more lives, 1,593 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: September 15, 2026, 06:44 PM
Seven more dengue patients died in the past 24 hours, while another 1,593 dengue patients were admitted to hospitals during the same period.
The Directorate General of Health Services’ (DGHS) Health Emergency Operations Centre and Control Room disclosed the figures in a statement issued today (Monday).
According to the report, 164 people have died of dengue so far this year. Of the deceased, 86 were women and 77 were men. Among the seven who died in the past 24 hours, four were women and three were men. They were aged between 21 and 75.
In the past 24 hours, 383 dengue patients were admitted to hospitals in districts under the Dhaka division. In addition, 290 were admitted in Khulna division, 171 in the Dhaka North City Corporation area and 165 in the Dhaka South City Corporation area.
Meanwhile, 159 patients were hospitalised in Chattogram division, 137 in Mymensingh, 132 in Rajshahi, 82 in Barishal, 65 in Rangpur and nine in Sylhet.
According to DGHS data, a total of 55,163 dengue patients have been admitted to hospitals across the country from January 1 to September 15 this year. Of them, 34,311 were men and 20,611 were women.
During this period, 50,941 patients recovered and were discharged from hospitals. Currently, 4,058 dengue patients are receiving treatment at hospitals across the country.
