Health 4 more die of dengue, 1978 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: October 1, 2026, 06:48 PM
Four more dengue patients have died in the past 24 hours, while 1,978 new dengue patients have been admitted to hospitals during the same period.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) said this in its regular dengue report on Thursday.
According to the report, among the newly admitted patients, 328 were admitted in Dhaka Division outside the city corporations, 180 in Dhaka North City Corporation and 176 in Dhaka South City Corporation.
In addition, 358 patients were admitted in Chattogram Division, 260 in Barishal Division, 256 in Khulna Division, 209 in Rajshahi Division, 101 in Rangpur Division, 94 in Mymensingh Division and 16 in Sylhet Division.
According to DGHS data, a total of 249 people have died from dengue in the country between January 1 and Thursday this year.
During the same period, a total of 81,598 dengue patients have been admitted to hospitals across the country. Of them, 75,699 have recovered and been discharged from hospitals.
