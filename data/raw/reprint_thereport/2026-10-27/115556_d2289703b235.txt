Health One more dies of dengue, 1163 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: October 2, 2026, 06:47 PM
One more dengue patient died in the past 24 hours, while 1,163 new patients have been admitted to hospitals with the disease during the same period.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) disclosed the information in its regular dengue report on Friday.
According to the report, the patient who died in the past 24 hours was undergoing treatment at a hospital in Dhaka division.
Among the newly hospitalised patients, 80 were admitted in Dhaka North City Corporation, 45 in Dhaka South City Corporation and 295 in areas outside the city corporations of Dhaka division.
In addition, 333 patients were admitted in Chattogram division, 151 in Barishal, 111 in Khulna, 77 in Mymensingh and 71 in Rangpur.
Since January 1 this year, a total of 250 people have died of dengue in the country. Of them, two died in January, two in February, one in May, 13 in June, 36 in July, 43 in August, 148 in September and five so far in October.
During the same period, a total of 82,761 dengue patients have been admitted to hospitals across the country. Of them, 76,862 have recovered and been discharged.
Currently, 5,649 dengue patients are receiving treatment at hospitals across the country.
