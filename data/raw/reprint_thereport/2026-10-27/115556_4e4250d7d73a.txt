Health 6 more die of dengue, 1948 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: September 30, 2026, 05:47 PM
Six more dengue patients died in the past 24 hours, while another 1,948 patients were admitted to hospitals with dengue during the same period.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) reported the figures in its regular dengue situation update released today (Wednesday).
According to the report, among the newly hospitalised patients, 343 were admitted in Chattogram Division, 320 in Dhaka Division outside the city corporations, and 248 in Khulna Division.
In addition, 212 patients were admitted in Barishal Division, 211 in Rajshahi Division, 210 in Dhaka South City Corporation, 189 in Dhaka North City Corporation, 102 in Mymensingh Division, 99 in Rangpur Division and 14 in Sylhet Division.
Of the six people who died in the past 24 hours, one died in Dhaka South City Corporation, one in Barishal Division outside the city corporation, one in Chattogram Division outside the city corporation, two in Khulna Division outside the city corporation and one in Rangpur Division outside the city corporation.
According to DGHS data, a total of 245 people have died of dengue in the country since January 1 this year through Wednesday. Of them, 114 deaths were reported in Dhaka Division, including the two city corporations.
So far this year, 47 dengue patients have died in Khulna Division, 24 in Chattogram Division, 20 in Mymensingh Division, 19 in Barishal Division, 14 in Rajshahi Division, six in Rangpur Division and one in Sylhet Division.
