Health 6 more die of dengue, 2010 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: November 3, 2026, 06:14 PM
Six more people died of dengue in the past 24 hours, while another 2,010 patients have been admitted to hospitals with the mosquito-borne disease during the same period.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) disclosed the information in its regular dengue update published today (Tuesday).
According to the report, Dhaka Division recorded the highest number of new hospital admissions, with 943 patients admitted in the past 24 hours.
Among other divisions, 245 patients were admitted in Chattogram, 235 in Khulna, 219 in Rajshahi, 219 in Barishal, 65 in Rangpur, 60 in Mymensingh and 24 in Sylhet.
With the latest fatalities, the total number of deaths from dengue in the country this year has risen to 439.
According to DGHS data, a total of 140,909 people have been hospitalised with dengue across the country between January 1 and November 3 this year.
Of them, 134,594 patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals.
