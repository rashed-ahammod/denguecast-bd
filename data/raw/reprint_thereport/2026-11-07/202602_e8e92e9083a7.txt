Health 6 more die of dengue, 1866 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: November 4, 2026, 05:48 PM
Six more people died of dengue in the past 24 hours, while 1,866 new patients have been admitted to hospitals during the same period.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) disclosed the figures in its regular dengue situation report released on Wednesday.
According to the report, Dhaka Division recorded the highest number of new hospital admissions, with 868 patients admitted in the past 24 hours.
Among other divisions, 256 patients were admitted in Chattogram, 215 in Khulna, 206 in Barishal, 171 in Rajshahi, 66 in Mymensingh, 71 in Rangpur and 13 in Sylhet.
With the latest fatalities, the death toll from dengue this year has risen to 445.
According to DGHS data, a total of 142,775 people have been hospitalised with dengue across the country between January 1 and November 4 this year.
Of them, 136,394 have recovered and been discharged from hospitals following treatment. Currently, 5,936 dengue patients are receiving treatment at hospitals across the country.
