Health 8 more die of dengue, 1,929 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: November 1, 2026, 05:56 PM
Photo: Collected
Dengue claimed eight more lives in Bangladesh in the 24 hours to 8 am today, while 1,929 patients were admitted to hospitals across the country, according to the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
The latest deaths have taken the country‍‍`s dengue toll to 427 so far this year.
Of the newly hospitalised patients, 831 were in Dhaka Division, 257 in Chattogram, 259 in Khulna, 185 in Rajshahi, 213 in Barishal, 92 in Mymensingh, 73 in Rangpur and 19 in Sylhet.
With the latest admissions, the total number of dengue patients hospitalised across the country this year has reached 136,929.
So far, 130,388 patients have recovered and returned home after receiving treatment.
Last year, dengue infected 102,861 people and claimed 413 lives across the country.
