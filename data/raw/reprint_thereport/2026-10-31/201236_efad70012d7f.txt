Health Dengue claims 9 more lives, 1982 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: October 28, 2026, 05:54 PM
Nine more people died of dengue in the past 24 hours, while 1,982 new patients have been admitted to hospitals across the country.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) disclosed the information in its regular dengue report released today (Wednesday).
According to the report, 1,982 people were admitted to hospitals with dengue between 8am Tuesday and 8am Wednesday.
Of the newly hospitalised patients, 816 were from Dhaka Division, 313 from Chattogram Division, 217 from Rajshahi Division, 214 from Khulna Division, 194 from Barishal Division, 116 from Rangpur Division, 84 from Mymensingh Division and 28 from Sylhet Division.
With the latest fatalities, the total number of deaths from dengue in the country this year has risen to 409.
According to DGHS data, a total of 130,219 people have been hospitalised with dengue across the country between January 1 and October 28 this year. Of them, 123,605 have been discharged after receiving treatment.
