Health Dengue claims 5 more lives, 2218 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: October 26, 2026, 05:49 PM
Five more people died of dengue in the past 24 hours, while another 2,218 have been admitted to hospitals with the mosquito-borne disease.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) reported the figures in its regular dengue update on Monday.
According to the report, 2,218 dengue patients were admitted to hospitals across the country between 8am Sunday and 8am Monday.
Of the newly hospitalised patients, 942 were from Dhaka Division, 284 from Khulna Division, 246 from Rajshahi Division, 240 from Chattogram Division, 232 from Barishal Division, 121 from Mymensingh Division, 113 from Rangpur Division and 40 from Sylhet Division.
With the latest fatalities, the total number of deaths from dengue in the country this year has risen to 389.
According to DGHS data, a total of 126,166 dengue patients have been hospitalised across the country since January 1 this year. Of them, 119,393 have recovered and been discharged from hospitals.
Over the past 24 hours, another 1,993 patients have recovered from dengue and been discharged from hospitals.
