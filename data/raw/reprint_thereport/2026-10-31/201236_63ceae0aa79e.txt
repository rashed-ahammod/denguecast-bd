Health Dengue claims 3 more lives, 1,262 hospitalised in 24 hours The Report Desk
Published: October 30, 2026, 06:59 PM
Photo/collected
Three more people have died of dengue in Bangladesh, while 1,262 were hospitalised with the mosquito-borne disease in the 24 hours to 8:00am today, according to the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
With the latest deaths, the country‍‍`s dengue death toll has reached 418 this year. The total number of hospitalised patients has also risen to 133,367.
Of the newly admitted patients, 435 were in Dhaka Division, 268 in Chattogram, 182 in Barishal, 131 in Khulna, 112 in Rajshahi, 75 in Mymensingh, 48 in Rangpur and 11 in Sylhet.
So far, 126,713 dengue patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals across the country.
Last year, dengue infected 102,861 people in Bangladesh and claimed 413 lives, according to DGHS figures.
