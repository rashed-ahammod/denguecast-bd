Eight more people died in dengue in last 24 hours in the country, while 2,036 fresh cases recoded in the disease, according to the health officials.
“Of the total, 254 patients were hospitalised in Barishal division, 323 in Chattogram division, 432 in Dhaka division, 169 in Dhaka South City Corporation (DSCC) and 206 in Dhaka North City Corporation (DNCC) areas, 267 in Khulna division, 115 in Mymensingh division, 197 in Rajshahi division, 62 in Rangpur division and 11 in Sylhet division, said a Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) statement.
Since the start of the dengue onslaughts, Bangladesh this year witnessed so far 233 deaths while the disease inflicted 75,730.
The dengue last year claimed 413 lives and inflicted 1,02,861 people in the country.
Besides, the dengue claimed 575 lives and inflicted 1,01,214 people in 2024.
