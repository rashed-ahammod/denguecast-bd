Three more deaths were reported from dengue in the past 24 hours till Monday morning, raising the number of fatalities from the Aedes mosquito-borne disease in Bangladesh to 85 this year.
In the 24-hour span, 994 more patients were hospitalised with viral fever, raising the total caseload to 29,923 in 2026.
Among the fatalities, all of whom were men, two were reported in Dhaka South City Corporation (DSCC) and one in Dhaka North City Corporation (DNCC), according to the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
Of the latest cases, 117 patients were admitted to hospitals in Barishal division, 107 in Chattogram division, 158 in Dhaka division, 97 under DNCC, 126 under DSCC, 215 in Khulna division, 71 in Mymensingh division, 76 in Rajshahi division, 23 in Rangpur division, and four in Sylhet division.
DGHS data shows that men account for 61.9% of infections so far this year, while women represent 38.1%.
Currently, 2,080 patients are receiving treatment in hospitals across the country.
According to official data, Bangladesh recorded 102,861 dengue cases and 413 deaths from the mosquito-borne disease in 2025.
