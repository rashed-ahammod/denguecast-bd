Three people have died from dengue across Bangladesh in the past 24 hours, while 372 new patients have been admitted to hospitals.
The Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) revealed the information on Monday through a media release.
According to the release, of the newly hospitalised patients, 67 were admitted in Barishal Division, 54 in Chattogram Division, 103 in Dhaka Division (outside the city corporations), 22 in Dhaka North City Corporation, 52 in Dhaka South City Corporation, 43 in Khulna Division, 21 in Mymensingh Division, four in Rajshahi Division and six in Rangpur Division.
The DGHS said that a total of 10,857 dengue cases have been reported so far this year. Of them, 60.9 percent are male and 39.1 percent are female.
A total of 9,786 patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals.
With the latest deaths, the total number of people who have died from dengue this year has risen to 37, it added.
