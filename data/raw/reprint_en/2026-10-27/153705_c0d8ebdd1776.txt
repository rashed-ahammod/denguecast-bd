Nine people have died due to dengue in the last 24 hours. At the same time, 1,954 people have been hospitalized due to dengue across the country.
This information was shared in a press release on dengue sent from the Health Emergency Operation Center and Control Room of the Directorate General of Health Services on Tuesday.
It said that among the patients hospitalized due to dengue in the last 24 hours, 196 are in Barisal division, 318 in Chittagong division, 465 outside the city corporation in Dhaka division, 189 in Dhaka North City Corporation, 191 in Dhaka South City Corporation, 230 in Khulna division, 97 in Mymensingh division, 184 in Rajshahi division, 71 in Rangpur division and 13 in Sylhet division.
As of October 13 this year, a total of 1,03,396 people have been hospitalized due to dengue across the country. A total of 97,296 dengue patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals so far this year.
Two thousand 965 people have been discharged in the last 24 hours. Nine people have died of dengue in the last 24 hours. A total of 316 people have died of dengue across the country so far this year.
