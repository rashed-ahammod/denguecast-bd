Six more people died in dengue in last 24 hours in the country, while 1942 people got hospitalized for the same issue, says the health officials.
“Out of 1,942, 213 patients were hospitalised in Barishal division, 301 in Chattogram division, 378 in Dhaka division, 229 in Dhaka South City Corporation (DSCC) and 178 in Dhaka North City Corporation (DNCC) areas, 264 in Khulna division, 120 in Mymensingh division, 197 in Rajshahi division, 56 in Rangpur division and six in Sylhet division, according to a Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) statement.
Since the start of the dengue onslaughts, Bangladesh this year witnessed so far 239 deaths while the disease inflicted 77,672.
The dengue last year claimed 413 lives and inflicted 1,02,861 people in the country.
Besides, the dengue claimed 575 lives and inflicted 1,01,214 people in 2024.
