One person has died from dengue in Bangladesh over the past 24 hours, raising the death toll from the disease this year to 250, according to government data released Friday.
A total of 1,163 people were also admitted to hospitals across the country with dengue during the same period, bringing the number of hospitalisations this year to 82,761.
The figures were reported by the dengue dashboard of the Directorate General of Health Services’ Health Emergency Operation Center and Control Room.
Of those admitted in the past 24 hours, 151 were from Barishal Division, 333 from Chattogram Division, 295 from Dhaka Division outside city corporations, 80 from Dhaka North City Corporation, 45 from Dhaka South City Corporation, 111 from Khulna Division, 77 from Mymensingh Division and 71 from Rangpur Division.
A total of 76,862 dengue patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals so far this year, including 1,163 in the past 24 hours.
