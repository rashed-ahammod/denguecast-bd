Three people have died from dengue and 251 others have been hospitalized with the mosquito-borne disease across the country in the past 24 hours.
The information was disclosed in a dengue update issued on Wednesday by the Health Emergency Operation Center and Control Room of the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
According to the DGHS, of the newly hospitalized patients, 66 were admitted in Barishal Division, 40 in Chattogram Division, 19 in Dhaka Division, 24 in Dhaka North City, 33 in Dhaka South City, 40 in Khulna Division, 15 in Mymensingh Division, 12 in Rajshahi Division, and 2 in Sylhet Division.
So far this year, 7,522 people have been hospitalized with dengue. A total of 6,875 patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals.
The death toll from dengue this year has now risen to 22.
