Six people have died of dengue in the country in the last 24 hours. At the same time, 1,655 people have been admitted to various hospitals in the country due to dengue.
With this, 190 people have died of dengue this year. A total of 63,627 people have been admitted to hospitals due to dengue this year.
This information was revealed on Monday from the dengue dashboard of the Health Emergency Operation Center and Control Room of the Directorate General of Health Services.
It said that among the patients hospitalized due to dengue in the last 24 hours, 217 are in Barisal division, 314 in Chattogram division, 281 in Dhaka division (outside the City Corporation), 166 in Dhaka North City Corporation, 167 in Dhaka South City Corporation, 271 in Khulna division, 43 in Mymensingh division, 146 in Rajshahi division, 41 in Rangpur division and 9 in Sylhet division.
And all six people who died of dengue in the last 24 hours are from Rajshahi division (outside the city corporation). Their ages range from 5 to 40, and no women or girls have died in the last 24 hours.
