Seven people have died in the country in the last 24 hours, from 8 am on Saturday to 8 am on Sunday, due to Aedes mosquito-borne dengue fever. This brings the total number of deaths this year to 384.
This information was conveyed in the dengue report of the Directorate General of Health Services on Sunday.
In the last 24 hours, 2,249 new dengue patients have been admitted to hospitals. As a result, the total number of dengue patients admitted to hospitals this year has reached 1,23,948. Of them, 1,17,400 have been discharged from the hospital.
According to the report of the Directorate General of Health Services, among the patients admitted to hospitals due to dengue in the last 24 hours, 211 are in Barisal division, 217 in Chittagong division, 605 outside the city corporation in Dhaka division, 227 in Dhaka North City Corporation, 201 in Dhaka South City Corporation, 297 in Khulna division, 155 in Mymensingh division, 224 in Rajshahi division, 82 in Rangpur division and 30 in Sylhet division.
And in the last 24 hours, two people have died in Dhaka North City Corporation, one in Dhaka South City Corporation, one outside the city corporation in Khulna division, two in Mymensingh division and one in Rajshahi division.
