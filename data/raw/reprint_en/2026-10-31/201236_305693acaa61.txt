Highest number of deaths in a single day in the current dengue season have been recorded as 11 people have died of dengue in the last 24 hours. At the same time, 2,071 people have been admitted to hospitals across the country due to dengue.
This information was given in a press release on dengue sent by the Health Emergency Operation Center and Control Room of the Directorate General of Health Services on Tuesday.
It said that among the patients admitted to hospitals due to dengue in the last 24 hours, 899 are in Dhaka division, 97 in Mymensingh division, 241 in Chittagong division, 250 in Khulna division, 229 in Rajshahi division, 112 in Rangpur division, 217 in Barisal division and 26 in Sylhet division.
It also said that 11 people have died of dengue in the last 24 hours. A total of 400 people have died of dengue across the country so far this year. In the last 24 hours, 2,071 people have been admitted to hospitals due to dengue across the country.
As of October 27 this year, a total of 128,237 people have been hospitalized due to dengue across the country. So far this year, a total of 121,423 dengue patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals. Currently, 6,415 people are hospitalized.
From January 1 to December 31, 2025, a total of 122,861 people were infected with dengue and a total of 413 people died of dengue.
From January to December 31, 2024, a total of 121,214 people were infected with dengue and a total of 575 people died of dengue.
