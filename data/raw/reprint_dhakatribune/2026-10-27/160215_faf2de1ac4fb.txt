One more person has died from dengue in the last 24 hours. During the same period, 23 people were hospitalized after being infected with dengue across the country.
This information was disclosed on Monday in a dengue-related press release issued by the Health Emergency Operation Center and Control Room of the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
According to the release, among those hospitalized in the last 24 hours due to dengue infection, three were from Barishal Division, three from Chittagong Division, seven from Dhaka Division, two from Dhaka North City Corporation and two from Dhaka South City Corporation, four from Khulna Division and two from Mymensingh Division (outside city corporation areas).
During this time, 30 dengue patients were discharged from hospitals. With this, a total of 1,135 dengue patients have been discharged from hospitals so far this year.
So far this year, 1,226 people have been infected with dengue, of which 64% are male and 36% are female. Four people have died from dengue this year.
From January 1 to December 31, 2025, a total of 102,861 people were infected with dengue, and 413 people died from the disease.
In 2024, a total of 101,214 people were infected with dengue, and 575 people died from the disease.
