Bangladesh reported 196 more dengue hospitalizations in the past 24 hours, bringing the total number of confirmed cases this year to 7,306, according to the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
Of the new patients, 66 were admitted to hospitals in Barisal division, 41 in Chittagong division, four in Dhaka division outside the city corporations, 19 in Dhaka North City Corporation, 29 in Dhaka South City Corporation, eight in Mymensingh division, six in Rajshahi division, two in Sylhet division, and 21 in Khulna division.
During the same period, 139 dengue patients were discharged after recovery, raising the total number of recoveries this year to 6,676.
According to DGHS data, men account for 61.5% of the reported dengue infections this year, while women make up the remaining 38.5%.
The mosquito-borne disease has claimed 19 lives so far this year.
Bangladesh recorded 102,861 dengue cases and 413 deaths in 2025. In 2024, the country reported 101,214 cases and 575 deaths.
