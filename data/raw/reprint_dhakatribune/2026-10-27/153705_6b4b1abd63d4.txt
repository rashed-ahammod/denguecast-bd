No deaths from dengue were reported, but 747 dengue patients were admitted to hospitals across the country in the last 24 hours, according to the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS).
Of the 747 patients, 25 were admitted in Barisal division, 180 in Chittagong division, 216 outside the city corporations in Dhaka division, 61 in Dhaka North City Corporation, 40 in Dhaka South City Corporation, 81 in Khulna division, 92 in Mymensingh division, and 52 in Rangpur division.
A total of 59,147 dengue patients have been hospitalized across the country this year. Of them, 54,838 dengue patients have recovered and been discharged from hospitals. Currently, 1,746 patients are undergoing treatment in hospitals.
A total of 176 people have died from dengue so far this year.
A total of 102,861 people were infected with dengue, while 413 people died of dengue in the last year.
